Calculation Methodology & Portfolio Disclaimers

• Gross Monthly Income Formula: (Hourly Rate × Hours Worked per Week × 52 Weeks) ÷ 12 Months.

• Net Income Estimation: Net estimates apply a user-adjusted effective tax & deduction rate (default 20%). Actual take-home pay depends on specific local tax brackets, state taxes, healthcare withholdings, retirement (401k) contributions, and employee benefits.

• 168-Hour Weekly Allocation: Total weekly time is strictly 168 hours. Free hours represent the remainder after subtracting weekly work hours and daily essential routines (sleep, cooking, commute, personal care).
